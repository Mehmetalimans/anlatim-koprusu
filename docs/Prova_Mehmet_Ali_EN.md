## A. 0:00-0:40 - Problem / Mehmet Ali

Hello, we are Group Fourteen.
Our project is Anlatım Köprüsü.
It helps university students understand difficult parts of their course texts.
A student may know the topic but still struggle with the words.
We show the original text and explain the difficult part.
Then we ask a short question and give feedback on the student's reason.
The app and learning materials are in Turkish.

## G. 3:25-4:00 - Other subjects / Mehmet Ali

We also prepared examples for Physics Two and basic Python.
Physics covers electric potential and potential energy.
We check the units and the signs.
Python covers for loops and range.
We check the code layout and output values.
The same learning process is designed for all three subjects.

## I. 4:35-5:00 - Social impact and limits / Mehmet Ali

Our goal is to make course texts easier to understand.
This connects our project to Sustainable Development Goal Four.
We have not measured learning gains.
AI can make mistakes, so we show the original text and review the demo results.
Thank you.
